How a bank alert becomes a payment

1
Your inbox receives the credit alert

A Gmail filter, a Postmark inbound rule or an SMS forwarder — whatever already gets the alert.

2
Your relay signs it and posts to the endpoint above

The relay holds the shared secret and mints the envelope. The gateway never parses raw mail itself.

3
The matcher resolves one order, or parks it for review

An unsigned alert can only ever land in review — never a delivery.
